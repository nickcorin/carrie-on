# Menu bar

Carrie On Dev offers an update to a newer local commit when available; source installations offer **Rebuild and Restart…**. Dev rebuilds the entire bundled app; source installations rebuild their menu app. See [rebuild usage](../USAGE.md#menu-bar) for progress, failure handling, and saved checkout behavior, and [distribution verification](distribution.md#verification) for isolated rebuild tests.

Chat lists use a narrow, softly tinted native scrollbar thumb without a visible track beside the cards. The thumb appears while hovering over its list or for 0.8 seconds after scrolling, and only when content overflows. AppKit retains native scrolling and thumb dragging. Verify both lists with overflowing and short results, then move the pointer outside the list and confirm the thumb disappears after scrolling stops.

Both sections use native virtualized lists. Cards retain their own actions and UUID identity without row selection. AppKit owns accessible page scrolling and beginning/end navigation; card spacing and section heights remain independent of the list's loaded item count.

Adding or removing a queue enrollment fades and gently scales its cards between sections over 0.3 seconds, while surrounding cards and section heights ease into place. Transitions follow confirmed queue changes, including background reconciliation; instruction edits and search do not trigger queue animations. Reduce Motion disables these transitions.

Cards are neutral for available chats, amber with a clock while queued, blue with a spinner while sending, and red with a warning icon when reactivation fails or needs attention. State icons sit beside the title on both faces; instructions stay plain. Hover help describes the state: **Scheduled for the next usage reset**, **Sending instruction…**, or the failure reason and recovery guidance. Sending cards retain a subtle two-second border pulse; Reduce Motion keeps both spinner and border static. Editing and removal are disabled while a delivery lease is active. The visible picker reads queue status every second independently of usage and chat metadata refreshes. Routine readiness checks do not change a card’s queued appearance; very fast sends can finish between reads.

The chat title is the primary heading. Queued cards place a single-line instruction preview directly beneath it, marked by a small quotation mark; hover reveals the full instruction. The smaller footer pairs the project with an explicit **Last active** time. It aligns with the main content’s horizontal inset and retains a modest bottom margin, separated by space rather than a divider. Available cards show only the title and context. All queue states share the same compact layout; failure details remain in hover help.

Red cards remain in Queued, retain their saved instruction, and show recovery guidance in hover help and accessibility. Retryable failures say **Couldn’t resume** and explain that they retry automatically. Busy chats, quota waits, and connection failures are retryable. Stale entries say **Conversation continued** and explain that their instruction will not be sent. Active ownership blocks edits/removal until expiry; idle failed entries remain editable. Enqueue, edit, and removal errors keep their separate action warning.

The fixture includes queued, sending, and stale-conversation states. `python -m unittest tests.test_chat_pagination tests.test_api tests.test_dispatch` checks native state mapping and readable status during worker processing.

Open **Carrie On** (or **Carrie On Menu** for source installations) from Applications. A single status item groups the monochrome C-arrow logo and queued-chat count, followed by a compact next-reset countdown, such as the mark followed by `3 · 2h 14m`. The custom AppKit template mark adapts to light and dark menu bars. The tooltip includes the exact reset time and usage. Clicking anywhere on the item opens the chat picker directly in an anchored popover, with a usage progress bar above the chat cards. The app refreshes passively every minute, on wake, and when opened; the visible popover updates in place. The settings menu shows the installed app version from its bundle metadata and offers Launch at Login, Check for Updates, and Quit Carrie On; Quit stops the app, worker, and local interface. See [startup and quit](app-lifecycle.md). See [built-in updates](../INSTALL.md#updates) for download, installation, and recovery. There is no manual refresh action.

Queued and Chats collapse independently and remember their expanded states. Each heading has a separator extending to the right edge, and its entire row toggles the section. A shared search field stays above both sections, including when collapsed, and filters titles in both as you type. Counts reflect matching results. Clearing the query restores both lists without changing queue membership. Queued chats occupy a separate scroll area above the available cards. Both sections sort by last activity, newest first, with unknown times last. Compact cards show titles, projects, and relative last activity (hover for the exact time), highlight on hover, and flip vertically to individual instruction editors when clicked. The placeholder and blank-editor default is **Please continue with your last task.** The instruction editor receives focus when a card flips. Enter submits and returns to the list; Shift–Enter inserts a new line, and Escape returns without submitting. The compact send-arrow **Add to queue** icon sits inside the editor; there are no selection checkboxes, Done control, or batch queue button. Clicking the title or blank body flips back without submitting. Drafts span searches.

Queued cards use amber styling and a clock beside the title, separate from the saved instruction. Hovering over the card front narrows its right edge and reveals a trash button for direct removal. The entire row remains the hover target, including the revealed button. Reduce Motion disables the animation. The instruction editor contains only its save control; removal is available from the card front. Send, save, and trash controls share hover and pressed highlights; destructive controls use red, and disabled controls stay subdued. Edits preserve reset deadlines and require a waiting enrollment whose saved instruction has not changed elsewhere. Removal preserves cancellation history. The usage bar contains its remaining-percentage label and blends continuously between system red at 0%, orange at 50%, and green at 100%. Color and fill changes animate over half a second, unless Reduce Motion is enabled. Its track carries a subtle matching tint, so an exhausted quota still appears red. A compact caption row above it reads **Usage remaining**, with the live reset countdown aligned on the right. Choose a usage bucket when required. Background reads and per-card operations show activity indicators without disabling unrelated controls. Warning icons expose failures on hover, and unconfirmed writes block only the affected card until reconciliation. Newer searches and mutations take precedence over older read responses. The tray has no dashboard navigation or worker-health banner; the settings menu contains the quit action. Per-card errors remain visible. It uses the local API service for chat and queue operations. Opening the app from Applications also opens the picker. Login startup uses `--background` to keep it hidden.

The popover inherits the system appearance and accent color, leaving its material to AppKit. AppKit supplies Liquid Glass where supported; compact queue and save controls sit inside the instruction editors. System motion and transparency preferences remain under macOS control.

Instruction edits use the [API contract](../openapi.json). They require the item UUID and its revision in `If-Match`. Active delivery blocks editing and removal; unrelated cards remain usable. Removal addresses the item UUID, so a stale card cannot cancel a replacement for the same chat. Creation persists the complete immutable request in profile-isolated preferences: UUID, chat, exact instruction, and optional quota bucket. Unknown outcomes survive app restarts. A confirmed matching item resolves the request; a 404 permits replay of that original request, even if the visible draft changed. Failed per-item reads and identity conflicts retain uncertainty and cannot silently start a new enrollment. Queue cards use item UUIDs for view identity; drafts and catalog metadata remain chat-scoped.

Run `python -m tools.install_menubar` after `just setup` to build, test, sign, install, and start it. The installer accepts `--port` for a nondefault local API. The Swift source lives in `menubar/main.swift` and `menubar/ChatPicker.swift`. Background polling reads `/api/v1/status`, which never includes chat contents or identifiers. The picker uses the existing local chat, status, and enrollment endpoints. See the [guide](../USAGE.md#menu-bar) for semantics, recovery, and removal.

The read-only `/api/v1/queue/metadata` endpoint fetches queued metadata independently of search, without loading chat turns. Unavailable metadata retains the enrollment title and reports unknown project and activity. Catalog reads request descending `updated_at` ordering from Codex.

The Chats section loads 25 summaries initially and requests the next page as its bottom comes into view. A `+` after the displayed count indicates more results are available; the count covers loaded matching chats. Pagination continues beyond 100 chats. Two subtle placeholder cards appear at the bottom while loading, with a 0.22-second transition that respects Reduce Motion. Failed pages retain existing cards and their cursor and expose a retry control. Search starts a fresh sequence of pages, and late responses from earlier searches are ignored immediately, including during the typing debounce. Queued chats and instruction drafts remain independent of pagination. Repeated chat IDs across live pages appear only once. The [API contract](../openapi.json) describes `/api/v1/chats`. Queue and metadata pages are also consumed to exhaustion, with duplicate item UUIDs suppressed.

Connection status and refresh activity share a compact footer below the lists, separated by a fine divider. While connected, background usage, chat, metadata, and connection checks replace the status dot with a small spinner while the “Connected to Codex” label stays unchanged. The indicator returns to the connected dot when those reads finish. Offline states retain their explanation during retries; local queue edits keep their per-card feedback. Chat refresh errors appear in the footer. The title has no separate refresh or error indicator. A green dot indicates a successful desktop handshake; disconnected states use a muted dot and a concise explanation, without hover text. When Codex is installed but closed, **Open Codex** launches its registered macOS application directly. The button shows **Opening…** while the launch is pending; a failed launch shows a retryable footer message. The launcher supports Codex hosted inside ChatGPT and never launches its helper process directly. Usage is hidden while disconnected. macOS app registration and running-app information distinguish **Codex isn’t open**, **Codex isn’t installed**, and **Waiting for Codex**. Both standalone Codex and Codex hosted inside ChatGPT are recognized. A local service failure has its own reconnecting message. Cached cards and saved instructions remain visible; queued instructions can still be edited and removed while Codex is offline. New enrollment waits for a connection. The app retries automatically every minute, on opening, on wake, and when Codex launches or terminates. A restored connection reloads the chat catalog without clearing drafts or queue membership.

The app refreshes connection status, usage, and queue summary together through `/api/v1/status`. Overlapping refreshes share a single request. The footer’s **Last updated** label records the last complete refresh. It shows **Just now** for the first minute, minutes ago for the first hour, then a local clock time without seconds; older dates include the date. Until a complete refresh succeeds, it shows **Never**. Partial reads, failed refreshes, and menu redraws preserve that timestamp. The footer has no hover text.

The read-only `/api/v1/connection` endpoint performs a desktop handshake with a three-second timeout, without opening chats or running inference. It returns a boolean availability observation, not a guarantee that a later dispatch will succeed. See [the connection API contract](../openapi.json). Native self-checks cover missing, closed, unresponsive, and connected states plus offline queue management; HTTP tests cover handshake loss and recovery while preserving queue data.

`python3 -m unittest -v` verifies metadata and partial failure behavior. The native binary's `--self-test` checks decoding, usage selection, future reset selection, unknown data, invalid usage, window labels, picker reconciliation, explicit bucket selection, activity formatting and ordering, shared search across both sections, and per-card action isolation without opening an app or contacting Codex. Installation runs this check before modifying the app bundle. Native click-through tests point the app at an isolated synthetic service, never a production enrollment.
